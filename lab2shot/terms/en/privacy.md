# Privacy Policy

The Service keeps only the information needed to run it and to investigate problems. This policy explains what is kept, for how long, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

## 1. What is kept

- **Account**: user name, display name, department, password (only a salted, irreversible hash is kept, never the password itself), creation and expiry times, role, the node categories you may use, and your quota. For a self-registered account, the time of registration, the IP address and the invite code used are also recorded.
- **Your data**: the footage you upload, the node graphs you submit for computation, the results and logs computed, and the node graphs you save on the server.
- **Usage records**: a record of each computation (when, which nodes were used, how long it took), and the amount of data you receive from the server each day.
- **Login and consent records**: the time, IP address, browser and operating system, and device identifier of each login (whether successful or not); the version of this Policy and of the Terms of Service you agreed to, with the time and IP address.
- **Service logs**: the server's operating logs contain user names, IP addresses and the actions performed.
- **Feedback**: the text and screenshots you submit with feedback, and the diagnostic information the web page attaches automatically (browser, node graph, web page log).
- **In your browser**: a cookie used for logging in, and a locally stored device identifier and page settings.

The footage you upload may contain sensitive personal information such as faces and bodies. It is used only for the computations you start yourself and for no other purpose. Before uploading, you must confirm that you have obtained the authorization of the people concerned (see the Terms of Service).

## 2. How long it is kept

- **Jobs** (node graphs, footage, outputs, logs): deleted in full {task_keep_days} days after a job ends; you can also delete your own jobs at any time under "Queue".
- **Uploaded footage**: deleted {task_keep_days} days after no job uses it any longer.
- **Account information and node graphs saved on the server**: kept for as long as the account exists.
- **Login records**: kept for {login_log_days} days. Service logs are rotated by size; when full, the oldest are deleted first.
- **The database** is backed up automatically every day, and the latest {database_backups} backups are kept; deleted data disappears from the backups only once those backups have been rotated out.
- **Computation records, feedback and records of administrative actions** are kept permanently; once an account is deleted completely, they can no longer be linked to you.

## 3. Who can see it

- Other users cannot see any of your data.
- The operator (administrator) may view all data in order to run the Service, investigate problems and handle complaints.
- All computation takes place on this machine; your footage is not sent to any external service. Your information is not sold or provided to third parties, except where required by laws and regulations or lawfully requested by a competent authority.
- The operator will do their best to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security. The operator is not liable for any disclosure or loss of your footage or information caused by hacking, theft of equipment, system failure or other accidents that are difficult to foresee or avoid. Do not upload confidential or unpublished footage.

## 4. Deleting your account

- To delete your account, contact the administrator (vfx_dli@foxmail.com). When an account is deleted, its queued and running jobs are stopped and its output results deleted, and its footage, cache and job folders are then cleaned up automatically. When an account is deleted completely, the account itself and the node graphs you saved on the server are deleted as well; only computation records, feedback and logs that can no longer be linked to you remain.
- To access, correct or delete your information, use "Send Feedback" in the editor, or email vfx_dli@foxmail.com.
- This Policy is provided in Chinese and in English. In case of any inconsistency between the two, the Chinese version prevails.
